Seek First Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Seek First handles information. Seek First is designed to work locally on your device using Apple's Screen Time APIs.

Summary

  • Seek First does not sell personal information.
  • Seek First does not use third-party advertising or third-party analytics.
  • Seek First does not require an account.
  • Seek First stores your app selections and reading goal locally on your device.

Information the App Uses

Seek First uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks so you can select apps, categories, or websites to hide, and select a reading or study app for your daily goal. These selections are represented by Apple-provided tokens. Seek First uses those tokens to apply restrictions and monitor progress on your device.

Information Stored on Your Device

Seek First stores app settings such as selected apps, selected reading app, start time, reading goal, and daily progress in local device storage and shared app group storage used by the app and its extensions.

Information Sent to the Developer

Seek First does not operate a server for user accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The app does not send your Screen Time selections, reading progress, or app usage data to the developer.

Apple Screen Time

Screen Time authorization, app selection, shielding, and activity monitoring are provided by Apple. Seek First uses these Apple APIs to perform the app's core features on your device.

Children's Privacy

Seek First does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account and does not transmit personal information to a developer-operated server.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Seek First's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact option listed on the Seek First App Store page.
